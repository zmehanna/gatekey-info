THE PRIVATEGATEKEY EXPERIENCE

One entrance. Clear permissions. Effortless access.

PrivateGateKey brings secure access and guest management together in one thoughtfully designed iPhone experience — while keeping safety and user control at its core.

01

Controlled gate access

Authorized users can request a gate action with an explicit confirmation step. A short anti-repeat safeguard helps prevent accidental double activation.

02

Individual invitations

Invite a guest or a booking manager without sharing administrator credentials. Permissions are assigned to the right account and managed centrally.

03

Connected device monitoring

See when the connected gate controller is online or offline. When physical position sensors are absent, PrivateGateKey does not guess whether the gate is open or closed.

04

Multiple account profiles

Switch between previously authenticated accounts, with supported biometric re-verification and clear visibility of the active session.

BUILT FOR REAL-WORLD STAYS

From the owner to the guest, everyone sees what matters.

Role-based access helps separate administration, reservation responsibilities and everyday entry, without making the experience complicated.

01 / ADMIN

Administrator

Manage access, authorized accounts, invitations and the connected gate experience.

02 / BOOKING MANAGER

Booking manager

Access the reservations and permissions specifically assigned to the booking manager.

03 / USER

Guest or resident

Use the gate access that has been granted, without unnecessary administration controls.
